# KU Car Club

The landing page for Khalifa University's car club. It is a plain static site: one HTML file, images and fonts. There is nothing to build or install.

## Files

- `index.html` – the page, with its styles and script inside it
- `assets/` – the logo, the two car sketches and the film-collection photos
- `fonts/` – the four typefaces the page uses, with their Open Font Licence texts

## Change the links

The join buttons open the club's WhatsApp invitation and "Get the meet alert" opens the club's Instagram. To change either, search `index.html` for `chat.whatsapp.com` or `instagram.com` and replace the address.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

In the repository, open Settings, then Pages. Under "Build and deployment", choose "Deploy from a branch", pick `main` and `/ (root)`, and save. The page appears at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/` a minute or two later.
